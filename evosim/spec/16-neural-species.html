16 — Neural species and the brain comparison

A neural species is an ordinary EvoSim species whose brain is a small neural network carried in its genome. It lives beside the JEV species, in the same worlds, under the same rules. The question it answers: does an evolved network survive better or worse than an evolved sentence genome read by an LLM? The design and its reasons are in Docs/Neuro/PLAN.md; this chapter is the reference for what was built. Code: Assets/EvoSim/Neuro (assembly EvoSim.Neuro, editor tools in EvoSim.Neuro.Editor).

1. What a neural animal is

Part Class What it does
Genome NeuralGenomeGene one locus holding every weight as a vector allele (vec[n]#hash, values in vectors.jsonl)
Network NeuralLayout, NeuralNet inputs from the senses in order, hidden layers (default one of 12, tanh), one linear output per action
Senses RayFanSense, InteroceptionSense 12 rays over 360° (one channel per kind of thing, 1 − distance/range), and energy, stamina, age, readiness, food, cover, the last action, ticks since a meal
Actions TurnAction, ForwardAction, StopAction left, right (20°), walk, run, stop; each eats, strikes or scavenges what is in reach, and breeds when ready
Brain NeuralBrain one forward pass per decision; Greedy (the largest output) by default, or Sample (softmax); from SyncBelow (32) queries on, on a worker task beside the other brains, with the same rows
Evolution NeuralMutation, NeuronCrossover adds or replaces weights at random; a baby takes each neuron whole from one parent
Stats HungerClock, NeuroStats the meal clock the interoception reads; speed, meals per tick and per meter, action shares, loops in stats.csv

Founders are random networks from a seeded pool, the same in every run (GENE-21). Rays stop at the first thing they meet: animals, food, carcasses, cover and the world's edge all occlude.

2. Matched to JEV

NeuralTemplates (menu GameObject ▸ EvoSim ▸ New Neural Species ▸ Prey | Predator) builds prey-nn ("neural prey") or predator-nn ("neural predator") as a copy of a JEV species: every body and life setting is kept, only the controller changes. MatchedPairs names the pairs and validation reports:

Code Severity Check
V-70 error a species with NeuralBrain has no NeuralGenomeGene
V-71 error founder vectors don't match the layout's length
V-72 error a neural sense's width changed since the layout was built (reported as a failed query at decision time, not before the run; 40 #21)
V-73 warning sexual mating without NeuronCrossover
V-74 info a neural species decides less often than every tick
V-75 warning a contact the diet can't use
V-76 error ray fan settings out of range
V-77 info the Sample policy in a World whose sampling τ ≠ 1
V-78 warning a matched pair differs in a body or life setting (each one is named)
V-79 warning a diet or a cover treats the two of a pair differently

S32 and S33 run asexual (with the JEV mating line "An adult that chooses mate breeds alone as soon as it is ready."), with grass regrowth ×2, seeds 1 and 2, and two arms: default (neural species decide every tick) and matched (every 4 ticks, staggered, as JEV). L0 variants replace JEV by the random brain and switch the JEV species' LLM mutation off. The JEV species keep their senses and prompts, so their answer caches stay valid; in Prey_vs_Prey the placeholders that named the removed predator are written as their values, so the JEV prey's prompt is exactly Lab1_Full's. The builders are EvoSim ▸ Neuro ▸ Build Neuro Scenes and Build Comparison Scenes.

4. Survival

  • SurvivalStats (world service) adds the sv_ columns to stats.csv and writes survival.json at the run's end (13: files and columns); NeuroStats adds each neural species' nn_ columns.
  • PlateauStop (a phase after Record) closes a window every 2 000 ticks and stops the run cleanly (RAND-20) at the first boundary where every tracked species (the ids listed, else every neural species) has a mean generation of at least 50 and its mean population, starvation hazard, predation hazard and births per animal stay within ±10 % over the last three windows. Cap: 60 000 ticks (reason plateau cap). With Stops Run off it only records the tick.
  • SurvivalAnalysis rebuilds each life from events.jsonl (founder, birth or newcomer to death; migration and the run's end are censoring) and computes Kaplan–Meier curves with Greenwood bands, death-rate ratios with a 95 % interval from the log rate ratio (SE = √(1/d₁ + 1/d₂)), per-window measures and the kill matrix.
  • SurvivalReportWindow (Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Survival Report) opens the runs of one scenario and arm and writes Logs/EvoSim/Reports/<scenario>-<variant>-<date>.md and .csv. Headline: the death hazard ratio neural/JEV per role over the late windows, per seed and pooled; an effect counts only when both seeds agree and the pooled interval excludes 1.

T-NEURO-01…16 (30 §3) in Assets/EvoSim/Neuro/Tests (assembly EvoSim.Tests.Neuro), plus the conformance suites, which pick up every neural sense, action, brain, gene and operator. T-NEURO-16 calls JEV: it runs only with EVOSIM_T4=1 in the environment.
