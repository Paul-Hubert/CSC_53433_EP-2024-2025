Extending EvoSim

Everything in EvoSim is a component you can add, remove, replace or subclass: senses, actions, genes, stats, death rules, mutation operators, brains, tick phases, food layers, cover, ground. The reference modules are written exactly like yours: they derive from the same public base classes and use no hidden shortcut. Any of them is a working example of its kind, and any of them can be replaced by your own.

The workflow

  1. Write a class. Assets ▸ Create ▸ EvoSim ▸ Script ▸ writes a template for a Sense, an Action, a Gene kind, a Stat, a Phase, a Brain or a Mutation operator into Assets/Student, together with a test in Assets/Student/Tests. For other kinds, derive from the base class named in the table below.
  2. Place the component. A module under a species belongs to that species. A phase or a service sits under the World, outside any species. Order matters only among modules of the same kind: the n-th action found, depth first, is action n. See Species modules and Phases, services and world hooks.
  3. Press Play. The World discovers your component, sets it up in its fixed order, and validates the tree. If something is missing, a message names the object to fix.
  4. Test it. Your sense, action, stat, layer, mutation operator, phase or locomotion runs through the conformance suite of its kind: Window ▸ General ▸ Test Runner, EditMode. A brain gets a test to copy instead. See Testing your module.

Five rules every module keeps

  1. Draw random numbers only from the stream you are given (a RandomStream), never from UnityEngine.Random, System.Random or the clock. Otherwise a seed no longer replays the run (RAND-01).
  2. Never decide by iterating a Dictionary or a HashSet: their order is not fixed. Iterate lists (RAND-05).
  3. Keep settings in the module and state in the animal. One module serves every animal of its species. What belongs to one animal goes in its stats, through the ids your module declared.
  4. Each kind of module does its own job. Senses only read. Actions only say where to go and what to do on arrival; the act phase moves the animal. Phases change the world. Views only display (SPACE-12).
  5. Set up in Initialize, not in Awake or Start. The World calls its modules in a fixed order (ARCH-04).
