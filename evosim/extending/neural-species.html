A neural species

EvoSim's brains need not read text. The EvoSim.Neuro assembly adds a species whose genome is the weights of a small neural network, evolved by mutation and crossover like any other gene. Use it as it is, beside the JEV species, or as a worked example of a brain that reads numbers: every part of it is an ordinary extension point.

Adding one

  1. Open a scene with a World that has a JEV prey or predator.
  2. GameObject ▸ EvoSim ▸ New Neural Species ▸ Prey (or Predator). The new species copies the selected JEV species' body and life settings and gets its own senses, actions, genome and brain.
  3. Add the new prey to the diets of the predators that should eat it. Validation warns (V-79) until you do.

The scenes Neuro_Lab, Neuro_Terrain_Small and Neuro_Terrain_Large are ready-made examples with neural species only (no JEV, no LLM, no model server); Brains_2x2 and Prey_vs_Prey put them beside JEV species; select a neural animal in Play mode and open Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Animal Inspector: its Network section shows the inputs and the outputs.

The parts, and the extension point each uses

Part Class Extension point
The weights NeuralGenomeGene a gene with vector alleles
Weight mutation NeuralMutation a mutation operator
Neuron crossover NeuronCrossover a crossover
Ray fan, interoception RayFanSense, InteroceptionSense senses with attachments (float arrays)
Turning, walking, stopping TurnAction, ForwardAction, StopAction self-directed actions
The forward pass NeuralBrain a brain that reads attachments and the genome, not text
Meal clock, loop statistics HungerClock, NeuroStats death rules that never kill, stats columns
Survival columns, stop rule SurvivalStats, PlateauStop a world service and a phase
The fan on screen RayFanView a view
The Network section NeuralInspectorPanel an AnimalInspectorPanel of the editor

Comparing brains

Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Survival Report reads the runs of S32 or S33 (one folder per seed) and shows how long animals of each species live, what kills them, and the hazard ratio of each neural species against its JEV match. The method is in chapter 16 of the interface documents.
