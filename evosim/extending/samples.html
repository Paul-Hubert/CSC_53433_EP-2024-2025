The samples

The samples are the recipes of 22 — Extending written out as working modules: thirst, water, drinking, seasons, slopes, a mutation operator without a model, a sense that tells a hunter from a neighbour, and a carcass that can be dragged away. Read them as complete examples of each mechanism, and add them to a scene to try them. They live in Assets/EvoSim/Samples, assembly EvoSim.Samples, namespace EvoSim.Samples. No reference scene uses them, so they change nothing until you put one in a world. Most are tested in Assets/EvoSim/Tests/EditMode/Samples/SampleTests.cs, and the senses, actions, stat, operator, phase and locomotion also go through the conformance suite of their kind.

To add a sample, select the GameObject named below, choose Add Component in the Inspector and type the class name. To change a sample, copy its file into Assets/Student and rename the class: the Student assembly sees the samples, so your class can also derive from one.

Thirst

Thirst declares a stat, thirst, on every animal of the species: 0 at birth, kept between 0 and Deadly (100). It adds a rule line to the prompt: "Thirst grows every step; an animal dies of thirst at 100. Drinking resets it." It does not change the stat itself: DiesOfThirst makes it grow and DrinkAction resets it.

  • Mechanism. A SpeciesModule that declares a stat in Declare and writes a rule in WritePromptRules. Other modules read it through Value: a[thirst.Value]. See Stats, costs and death rules.
  • In a scene. On a child of the species, next to its other stats. Settings: Per Tick (0.5), Deadly (100).
  • To let the brain feel thirst, add a LevelSense with Stat thirst: no code needed.

DiesOfThirst

DiesOfThirst runs in the death phase, once per animal per tick. It adds Per Tick to the animal's thirst, then returns the cause "thirst" when thirst has reached Deadly. At the reference values, an animal that never drinks dies after 200 ticks. The cause gets its own column in stats.csv (deaths_thirst, prefixed with the species id).

  • Mechanism. A DeathRule: Causes names its causes and CauseOfDeath returns one, or null to live on. [RequiresModule(typeof(Thirst))] (RequiresModuleAttribute) names the module it needs.
  • In a scene. On a child of the species (its Life object, beside Starvation). It needs a Thirst.

NearestWaterSense

NearestWaterSense tells the brain how far the nearest water is: here within 0.5 m (V2: "I am at the water's edge."), one token per distance band, or none. It asks the ground with NearestWater.

  • Mechanism. A BandedSense: the base class builds the tokens and writes the texts; the sample turns on the here token (HasHere) and maps a distance to a token in Read. See A sense.
  • In a scene. On a new child of the species' Senses object: its place among the senses is its place in the situation text. It needs a ground that knows water: the TerrainGround (ground below its Water Level is water). On a flat ground it always says none. It multiplies the species' number of possible observations by 6.

DrinkAction

DrinkAction gives the brain a new choice, drink: walk to the shore of the nearest water in sight, and reset thirst to 0 on arrival. With no water in sight the animal searches (ACT-04).

  • Mechanism. An AnimalAction that only states an intent and an interaction: the act phase moves the animal, then applies a small IInteraction (reach 0.5 m) that sets thirst to 0. Its IsRelevant reads the Water sense. See An action or an interaction.
  • In a scene. On a new GameObject under the species' Actions, named drink (the action's name in the prompt and its gene's label), with a TextGene on the same GameObject. It needs a Thirst on the species and a ground with water.

IntensityLadder

IntensityLadder mutates a text gene without a model: it finds the first of never, rarely, sometimes, often, always in the sentence and moves it one step up or down, keeping its capital and punctuation. "Rest often when tired." becomes "Rest always when tired." or "Rest sometimes when tired.". A sentence without such a word fails the job, and the gene stays as it was.

  • Mechanism. A MutationOperator: Accepts takes text genes, and StartMutation draws its one coin from the stream it is given (MUT-30). It sees nothing but the gene (CORE-07). See Mutation.
  • In a scene. On the species' Mutation object, beside the LlmMutation. Drag the genes it should handle into its Only These list. A gene goes to the operator that lists it; the other genes go to the first operator whose list is empty (MUT-02).

SeasonsPhase

SeasonsPhase splits time into years of Year Length ticks (2 000). In the second half of each year, winter, the food layer named in Layer ("Grass") regrows at Winter Regrow Factor (0.2) times its normal rate.

  • Mechanism. A TickPhase: Initialize finds the layer, Run sets its regrowth factor every tick, and the environment phase, later in the same tick, uses it (TICK-02). Validate reports a missing layer (V-20) and a wrong place (V-08). See Phases, services and world hooks.
  • In a scene. On a new GameObject under the World's Phases, above Environment: phases run in hierarchy order.

ChasedSense

ChasedSense answers yes when the nearest threat in sight is doing an action that hunts and its target is this animal, no otherwise. A cannibal, which flees its own kind, can then tell a hungry neighbour from a peaceful one.

  • Mechanism. A plain Sense with two tokens. It reads the hunter's action through Hunts and its target through CurrentTarget, both as they were at the start of the tick (SENSE-04), and writes its own V2 sentences.
  • In a scene. On a new child of the species' Senses. It only says yes in a species that has threats. It doubles the number of possible observations.

SlopeLocomotion

SlopeLocomotion moves like the kinematic locomotion, but refuses a step onto ground steeper than Max Slope Degrees (35) and charges Energy Per Meter Climbed (0.3 points) for every meter of height gained. Going down costs nothing extra.

  • Mechanism. A subclass of a reference module (MOVE-07): it overrides Move to change the intent before calling the base, and ExtraCost, which the metabolism adds to the cost of the move. See A locomotion.
  • In a scene. It replaces the species' KinematicLocomotion on its Locomotion object: a species has exactly one locomotion (V-13). Copy Walk Speed, Run Speed and Prompt Rule from the old one. It matters only on a ground with heights.

DragAction

DragAction walks to the nearest carcass in sight; within Grab Reach (1 m) it walks toward the nearest cover and drags the carcass along, at walking speed, until both are hidden. It is not a recipe of 22: it writes out scenario S18 of 31 — Scenarios, an entity that moves (ENV-21).

  • Mechanism. An action whose interaction moves an entity: the carcass changes position in the act phase, never from the views. Its interaction's reach is unlimited, so it applies wherever the animal ends its move.
  • In a scene. On a new GameObject drag under the actions of a species whose diet eats carcasses (the predator), with a TextGene. It needs a cover layer, and reads the Carcass sense.

Worked example: thirst on the terrain

The Terrain_Locomotion scene has a 96 m terrain whose lowest ground, about 15 %, is water. Four samples together make its prey thirsty. Do this in a copy of the scene.

  1. Open Assets/EvoSim/Scenes/Terrain_Locomotion.unity and save it as Assets/Student/ThirstyTerrain.unity.
  2. Select Terrain World. Unless the JEV server runs, set Default Brain to Brains/Random.
  3. Select Terrain World/Prey/Stats (it holds Energy, Stamina and Metabolism) and add a Thirst. Keep Per Tick 0.5 and Deadly 100.
  4. Select Prey/Life and add a Dies Of Thirst.
  5. Under Prey/Senses, create an empty child named Water, below Food, and add a Nearest Water Sense.
  6. Optionally, create a Thirst child below it with a Level Sense: Stat thirst, Low 30, High 70, and Words "I am not thirsty.", "I am thirsty.", "I am parched." (low thirst comes first).
  7. Under Prey/Actions, create a child named drink and add a Drink Action and a Text Gene. In the gene's Founders, write "Drink whenever you pass water." and "Drink only when very thirsty."; keep Has Neutral on. Contrast Pro and Contrast Anti ("Always drink, whatever happens." / "Never drink unless parched.") are optional: only brain tests use them.
  8. Press Play. The validation panel may warn that the prey's signature changed (V-12; Accept it) and that its observation space is large (V-50). Warnings don't stop the run.
  9. Open Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Event Log: deaths with the cause thirst appear from about tick 200, for prey that found no water. In the run's stats.csv the column prey_deaths_thirst counts them.
  10. To see what drinking is worth, disable the drink GameObject and run the same seed again, then compare the deaths by thirst. Scenario S16 makes the same comparison with a scripted brain.

Then try the others on the same scene: a Seasons Phase above Phases/Environment, or a Slope Locomotion for the predator only, with a Max Slope Degrees below the ground's 35°, so the two species cross the hills differently.

Test it

The same combination, in a test world with a pond (PondGround): a thirsty prey 2 m from the shore drinks within three ticks. See Testing for the helpers.

public class ThirstyPondTests : WorldFixture
{
    [Test, Description("22 §3: drink walks to the shore and resets thirst on arrival")]
    public void DrinkResetsThirst()
    {
        var w = New().Ground<PondGround>(20, 20).Food(0f, 0f).Phase<ActPhase>()
            .Species("prey", s => s.PreyBody().Action<DrinkAction>("drink").Module<Thirst>()).Build();
        var sp = w.FindSpecies("prey");
        var a = Place.Animal(sp, Place.At(10f, 15f));
        var thirst = sp.Module<Thirst>().Value;
        a[thirst] = 50f;
        a.Choose("drink");
        w.Advance(3);
        Assert.AreEqual(0f, a[thirst], "drank");
        Assert.IsFalse(w.Ground.IsWater(a.Position), "on the shore, not in the water");
    }
}
