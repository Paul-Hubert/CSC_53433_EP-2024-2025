Animals, stats and death

An animal is plain data: an Animal record owned by its species, not a GameObject. It holds where the animal is, what it is doing, and its numbers: stats that change during its life (energy, stamina) and traits fixed at birth (speeds, maximum stamina). Modules declare the stats and traits they need, so a species without a stamina module has no stamina at all (ANIM-11). This page covers the record, the reference stats, what a tick costs, busy states and how animals die.

Each module declares what it needs in Declare, on the species' SpeciesBuilder, before any animal exists. The ids it gets back are how it reads and writes the values later. Declaring a name that already exists returns the first declaration (V-38 warns if they differ). Anyone can look a declaration up by name on Species.Declarations.

A stat starts at a fixed value (Fixed(float)) or full, at a trait's value (FullTrait(TraitId)). Its maximum can be a trait: stamina is capped by each animal's own stamina.max. A trait starts at its default, the module's setting; a number gene may replace or scale it (ANIM-15). Modules always read the trait, never their own setting, so a gene changes behaviour with no extra code (ANIM-16). See Genes and mutation.

Energy and stamina

Energy declares the stat energy. Founders and newcomers start at 60, babies get the energy their parents pay for them (40, the litter's Child Energy), and the maximum is 100: a meal never raises it higher. It has no minimum. At 0 or below, the animal starves at the next death check.

Stamina is the number of meters an animal can still move. It declares the traits stamina.max (prey 60, predators 30) and stamina.regen (2 per tick), and the stat stamina, which starts full. Each meter moved costs one point; the locomotion never moves an animal farther than its stamina (ANIM-21). A tick without movement (less than 1 mm, ANIM-23) brings back stamina.regen points, up to the maximum, and costs 0.3 energy while stamina is below full.

Metabolism: what a tick costs

Metabolism charges every living animal once per tick, right after it acted (ANIM-20), from what it did: the meters its locomotion reported, its action, and whether it was busy at the start of the tick.

Situation (reference values, prey and predators) Energy Stamina
moved d meters −0.7 − 0.5 × d −d
still, with the rest action −0.2 +2, up to the maximum
still, any other action −0.7 +2, up to the maximum
still and busy (digesting) −0.2 +2, up to the maximum
while stamina recovers (still, not full) −0.3 more —
graze one food item +25 —
a kill +60 —
a carcass portion +30 —

So walking 1 m costs 1.2 energy, a predator running 2 m costs 1.7, and a predator can run at full speed for 15 ticks from full stamina. A locomotion may add a cost of its own for the move (slopes, mud), and the metabolism may also charge a cost that grows with a trait (off in the reference; see Stats, costs and death rules).

Busy states and digestion

While an animal is busy it doesn't decide and doesn't move; it pays the busy cost and recovers stamina (ANIM-30). A module that makes an animal busy sets BusyTicks and gives a reason, shown in the inspector (ANIM-31). The act phase counts down at the end of each busy tick. At 0 the animal has no action, and it decides at the start of the next tick.

In the reference only Digestion does it, on the predator. After a kill or a carcass portion the eater is busy for round(Digest Ticks × gain / Reference Gain) ticks: 50 after a kill (+60), 25 after a portion (+30), with the reason digesting. Grazing never makes an animal busy, and the prey has no digestion module.

Age, maturity and generations

Every living animal ages by one tick per tick, in the death phase, babies born that tick included (ANIM-35). An animal is an adult when its age reaches its maturity trait (150 ticks, declared by the MatingRule, ANIM-36); an adult with at least 50 energy is ready to mate. Founders and newcomers are generation 0; a baby is one generation more than the larger of its parents' generations. See Reproduction and population.

Death

The death phase (DeathPhase) runs once per tick:

  1. Animals killed this tick die, with the cause killed and the killer's id.
  2. Every living animal ages by one tick.
  3. Each DeathRule of the species is asked about every living animal, in module order. The first rule that names a cause decides; later rules are not asked about that animal.
  4. Each death is recorded once (ANIM-42), and the dead leave their species before the next tick's decisions (ANIM-43).
Cause Rule When (reference) stats.csv column
starvation Starvation energy ≤ 0 <species>_deaths_starve
old_age OldAge age above Max Age (1 500 ticks) <species>_deaths_age
killed a strike (ANIM-41) killed by a hunter <species>_deaths_pred
migrated the cap rule (mode Migrate), in the migration phase the species is above its cap <species>_deaths_migrated

Modules may add causes of their own, such as thirst; each gets a <species>_deaths_<cause> column (ANIM-40).

In the editor

  • Animal Inspector (Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Animal Inspector): in Play mode, click an animal in the Scene view to read its stats, traits, genome, current action, busy reason, parents and offspring.
  • Species inspector: select a species and press Show the computed tables to list every declared stat and trait, with its default, its range and the module that declared it.
  • Ecology Monitor (Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Ecology Monitor): population and deaths by cause over time.
