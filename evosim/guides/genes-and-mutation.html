Genes and mutation

An animal's behaviour comes from its genes: short English sentences the brain reads before every decision, and sometimes numbers that set a trait such as maximum stamina. A baby inherits a mix of its parents' genes, and now and then one gene mutates: a second language model, the mutator, rewrites the sentence. Selection does the rest. This page explains how genes are stored, expressed and changed. How parents and babies meet is in Reproduction and population.

Genes, loci and alleles

A gene is a component under a species, a Gene. Each gene is one locus: one slot in the genome of every animal of the species. The value an animal carries in that slot is an allele. Two animals with the same sentence at the same gene carry the same allele.

Gene kind Holds Expressed as
TextGene a sentence, e.g. "Run from any predator you see." a line of the prompt's genes block (GENE-03)
NumberGene a number, e.g. 60 a trait of the animal, e.g. stamina.max (GENE-04)

A text gene placed on an action, or below it, is bound to that action and takes its name as label (GENE-05). Each reference action prefab (Eat, Flee, Rest…) carries its own text gene, so the prey's flee gene is labelled flee. A text gene under no action must have Free ticked: it appears under its own label, for a temperament or a general rule.

The species collects its genes depth first in the hierarchy. That order is the locus order (Locus), the same in every genome. Each gene has a locus id <species id>.<label>, unique in the world: prey.flee, predator.hunt (GENE-11).

Alleles and the registry

Every allele seen in a run is registered once, in Alleles (an AlleleRegistry, GENE-10). Sentences are compared after collapsing spaces; numbers at the gene's Decimals (4 by default). Each Allele gets an id <locus id>:<n>, counted from 0 per locus: prey.flee:0, prey.flee:1… It also records where it came from (GENE-12):

Field Example
Origin founder, neutral, control, mutant
ParentId prey.flee:2, for a mutant of that allele
Operator llm#5 (instruction 5 of the deck), gauss
Model, Seed gemma4:26b and the call's seed, for a sentence written by the mutator

Following parent ids back from a mutant gives its lineage, down to a founder. The recorder writes every allele to alleles.jsonl (Recording and outputs).

Founder pools

Each gene has a founder pool, FounderPool (GENE-20). The founders at tick 0, and the newcomers that arrive below a species' floor, draw each locus independently and uniformly from it.

  • A text gene's pool is its founder sentences, plus the neutral allele "No preference." unless Has Neutral is off (GENE-24). The reference sentences live in shared AllelePool assets, one per locus, in Assets/EvoSim/Data/AllelePools (prey_flee.asset, predator_hunt.asset…).
  • A number gene's pool is its founder values: 45, 60 and 75 for the reference stamina gene.

Pools are frozen for an experiment (GENE-21): the World registers them before anything else, so founder alleles have the same ids in every run. Founder sentences pass the same guards as mutants (GENE-22): at most 12 words, ASCII letters, digits, spaces and , . ' ’ ; : ! ? - (SentenceGuards).

Genomes and expression

A Genome holds one allele per locus, in locus order (GENE-06). It never changes: a baby gets a new one. Its Key hashes the species and every allele value (GENE-13); its BrainKey hashes only the genes the brain reads, and is part of the memo and cache keys (DEC-31).

Express turns an allele into what acts, through an Expression. It runs at two moments:

  • at birth, a number gene sets its trait, or multiplies the trait's default, clamped to the trait's range;
  • when a decision's prompt is written, each gene the brain reads adds one line to the genes block (PROMPT-02).

For a reference prey, the genes block looks like this:

- eat: "Eat whenever food is close."
- flee: "Run from any predator you see."
- hide: "No preference."
- follow: "Keep your distance from other animals."
- rest: "Rest only when you feel safe."
- mate: "Mate with any nearby adult."

A number gene stays out of the prompt unless its Show In Prompt is on.

Mutation

flowchart LR
    C[Conception] --> R{Rate draw}
    R -->|no| K[Inherited allele]
    R -->|yes| O[Operator draws its choices]
    O -->|number| J[Job done]
    O -->|text| S[Mutator service]
    S --> M[Mutator model] --> G{Clean and guards}
    G -->|rejected, tries left| S
    G -->|5 rejected| K
    G -->|accepted| J
    J --> H{Hatching: gene check}
    H -->|ok| A[New allele]
    H -->|refused or unchanged| K
  1. Which loci. In the breed phase, after each baby's crossover, each locus mutates with the probability Rate of its operator: 0.03 per gene per baby (MUT-01). The draw comes from the species' mutation stream.
  2. Which operator. Exactly one MutationOperator applies to a locus: the first one that accepts the gene and lists it in Only These, else the first one that accepts it and lists no gene (MUT-02). The reference uses LlmMutation for text genes and GaussianMutation for number genes.
  3. Draw now. The operator draws all its random choices at conception (MUT-30) and returns a MutationJob. A Gaussian job is done at once: v′ = clamp(v + N(0, σ)) within the gene's range, σ = 5 (MUT-20).
  4. Ask the mutator. An LLM job goes to the World's MutatorService, described below.
  5. Hatch. The egg hatches once its jobs are done (MUT-32). Each new value must pass the gene's Check; it is then registered as a mutant and listed in the baby's birth event (MUT-03). A failed job, a refused value or a value equal to the parent leaves the inherited allele (MUT-04).

The LLM mutator

The deck is a text file of instructions, one per line; lines starting with # are ignored (MUT-10). The reference deck Assets/EvoSim/Data/Decks/mutate_v4.txt has seven, such as "Make the rule in this sentence a little weaker." and "Change when this rule applies.". For each of its 5 tries, LlmMutation draws an instruction and a seed at conception. The prompt is exactly (MUT-11):

The sentence below is a rule that a wild animal follows.
Make this rule say the opposite.

"Run from any predator you see."

Reply with the new sentence only.

The mutator sees nothing else: no animal, no world, no other gene (CORE-07).

  • Batches. The service sends all first tries of a tick as one batch at the end of the breed phase, then the redraws as later batches (MUT-31), through the World's MutatorClient. The reference client, OllamaMutatorClient, asks gemma4:26b in Ollama (http://localhost:11434) on the CPU, so the GPU stays the brain's; temperature 1.2, thinking off.
  • Cache. Answers are stored in the AnswerCache under the model, prompt, seed and temperature (MUT-14): a repeated run gets the same mutants without a model call.
  • Cleaning and guards (MUT-12, MutationText). The service keeps the quoted part, drops a leading "Here is …:", keeps the first sentence, adds a final period and a capital. It rejects the answer as invalid (no word, more than 12 words, the parent again, a forbidden character) or unchanged (only punctuation differs).
  • Redraws. A rejected try is followed by the next one, with its own instruction and seed (MUT-13). After 5 rejected tries the gene stays as it was.
  • No mutator. If the server can't be reached, the gene stays unchanged (MUT-04). With Strict on, the run also stops at the end of the tick.

A real mutation: "Run from any predator you see." → "Run quickly from any predator you see."

Eggs and waiting

The mutator takes time, so babies wait as eggs until their genome is complete. With the reference incubation of 0 ticks, eggs hatch in the tick they are laid, and that tick waits for the mutator's answers. An Incubation of a few ticks lets the world go on while the answers arrive (MUT-33). Either way the result is the same: every choice was drawn at conception, so it never depends on when an answer comes back.

The main types

Type Role
Gene the base class of a locus: label, kind, founder pool, expression, allowed values
TextGene, NumberGene the reference gene kinds
AllelePool an asset of founder and contrast sentences shared by several text genes
Allele, AlleleValue one registered value with its lineage; the value itself
AlleleRegistry every allele of the run, once per locus and value
Genome one allele per locus, in locus order
Expression what Express writes into: a trait, or a prompt line
MutationOperator the base class of a mutation rule; LlmMutation, GaussianMutation
MutationJob one mutation of one gene of one baby: done, failed or still running
MutatorService, MutatorClient batches, cleans and caches LLM mutations; sends the prompts to a server
MutationStats the run's counts: attempts, successes, model calls, rejections by reason (MUT-05)

On the World, the controls of 15 — Controls change genes for a whole run: No Mutation (C2) sets every rate to 0; Shuffled Genes (C3) makes each decision read the genes of another living animal of the species; Random Founders (C4) draws founder sentences from the World's Control Sentences and founder numbers uniformly in each gene's range.

In the editor

  • The species inspector's Genes foldout lists the genes in locus order, with their locus id, kind, founder count and the operator that applies, with its rate.
  • Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Gene Pool, in Play mode: pick a species and a locus. For a text gene it shows the allele shares over time (one colour per allele id), the 12 most common alleles with their text, origin and id, and, when you click one, its lineage back to a founder. For a number gene it shows a histogram of the living animals' values. It samples every 20 ticks while it is open.
  • GameObject ▸ EvoSim ▸ Add Gene ▸ Stamina gene adds the reference number gene with its Gaussian operator.
  • Assets ▸ Create ▸ EvoSim ▸ Mutation Deck writes a copy of the reference deck for you to edit.
  • Validation reports bad founders (V-40, V-42), empty pools (V-41), a missing neutral allele (V-43), a number gene's unknown trait or missing cost (V-45, V-47), a gene no operator accepts (V-48), a missing deck (V-20) or mutator (V-63).
