The environment

Everything in a world that is not an animal is part of the environment: the ground animals stand on, the food that grows on it, the cover they hide in, and entities such as carcasses and eggs. Each is a service, a component under the World's Ground and Environment objects. A world may have none, one or several of each kind (03 — Environment). This page explains the reference modules and their settings.

How it fits together

flowchart LR
    G[Ground: height, walkable, distance] --> F[FoodGrid: cells that may hold food]
    G --> C[CoverLayer: thickets]
    C -->|no food in cover| F
    K[Strike kills] --> CS[CarcassSystem]
    B[Breed phase] --> ES[EggSystem]
    EP[Environment phase] -->|Regrow| F
    EP -->|UpdateEntities| CS
    EP -->|UpdateEntities| ES
  1. When the World initialises, the ground comes first, since everything else is laid on it. The cover layer then builds its map of thickets.
  2. Once every module is ready (Begin), the food layer picks the cells that may hold food, outside water and cover, and places the first items.
  3. During a tick, senses and actions query the layers, the cover and the entities. The act phase removes eaten items at once, and a successful strike leaves a carcass.
  4. In the environment phase, the ninth phase of a tick, every resource layer regrows and every entity system ages its entities and removes the used-up ones. Nothing else changes the environment.

The main types

Type Role
Ground The world rectangle: height and walkability of every point, and the world's distance function
FlatGround Lab 1's ground: a flat rectangle, walkable everywhere
TerrainGround A Unity Terrain: water, steep slopes and trees are not walkable
ResourceLayer Edible items spread over the ground; FoodGrid is the reference ("Grass")
Cover Places where animals hide from their hunters; CoverLayer is the reference ("Thickets")
EntitySystem<T> Objects with a position that are not animals: CarcassSystem, EggSystem
Edible Makes a species, a layer or an entity kind edible, and says how much energy it gives
EnvironmentPhase Regrows the layers and updates the entities, once per tick

The ground

The ground answers three questions for any point: its height, whether an animal may stand there, and how far it is from another point. Distances are measured on the horizontal plane, straight line, in meters (SPACE-02), and every module measures through World.Distance. The world is a bounded rectangle with hard borders: nothing ever leaves it (SPACE-03).

FlatGround is a rectangle from (0, 0) to (Width, Depth) at height 0, walkable everywhere inside. The reference sizes are 192 × 192 m (Lab1_Full), 64 m (HideVsFlee) and 48 m (Lab1_Small).

TerrainGround reads a Unity Terrain on its GameObject or below it. Walkability is computed once, on a grid of Cell Size meters (1 m), from the centre of each cell:

Setting Reference Effect
Water Level set per scene a cell at or below this world height is water: not walkable
Max Steepness 35° a steeper cell is not walkable
Tree Footprint 0.5 m cells around each tree instance are not walkable (0 = trees don't block)
Keep Largest Region on walkable pockets cut off from the largest region become non-walkable (SPACE-05)

Heights come from the heightmap itself, not from the grid. A terrain ground also finds water: NearestWater returns a WaterHit with the water point and a walkable Shore point next to it, where a drinking animal stands.

Moving on the ground

Locomotions never move an animal off walkable ground (MOVE-02). The kinematic locomotion uses two helpers:

  • SlideTo moves in a straight line. If a border, water or an obstacle is in the way, it slides along it: it tries the point clamped into the rectangle and the two one-axis moves, and keeps the one that gets farthest in the wanted direction. If none gets anywhere, it stops at the last walkable point of the line, checked every 0.25 m.
  • SideStep frees a stuck animal: it tries up to 8 random directions, drawn from the species' actions stream, and takes the first clear one.

Founders and newcomers are placed at uniformly random walkable points, drawn from the species' founders stream.

Food: resource layers

A resource layer holds edible items. The reference FoodGrid keeps at most one item per cell, at the cell centre (03 §1):

Setting Reference Meaning
Cell Size 1 m the side of a food cell
Initial Fraction 0.1 share of eligible cells holding an item at the start
Regrow P 0.0015 probability per empty eligible cell per tick of growing an item: about 670 ticks to regrow an eaten cell
No Food In Cover on cover cells hold no food at the start and never regrow ("hungry cover", ENV-04)
Near Water Bonus 2 regrowth multiplier near water (terrain only)
Near Water Radius 3 m the distance to water that counts as near

A cell is eligible when its centre is walkable and, with hungry cover, outside cover. Food appears uniformly at random over the eligible cells. The layer's Edible gives 25 energy points per item in the reference.

An animal grazes an item when it ends its move within the act phase's Graze Reach (0.5 m) of it. Eating removes the item at once: two animals never eat the same item, and the act order decides who gets a contested one (ENV-02).

Another phase can change how fast food grows: RegrowFactor multiplies the regrowth probability (1 by default). The seasons sample (Assets/EvoSim/Samples/SeasonsPhase.cs) sets it to 0.2 in winter, from a phase placed before the environment phase (The samples).

Cover

The reference CoverLayer makes thickets: the top Fraction of walkable cells (20 %) of a value-noise map with two octaves, 8 m and 4 m, on cells of Cell Size (1 m). The thickets come in clustered patches. They never change during a run.

An animal standing in cover is hidden from the species that hunt it (ENV-11): its hunters' senses don't report it, their actions can't target it, and a strike on it fails. Its own species and the species it hunts still see it. Hiding Species limits hiding to a list of species ids (empty = every species).

A world may have several covers: an animal is hidden when any of them hides it, and the hide action runs to the nearest cover point of any of them. A world without cover behaves as if no point were in cover (ENV-12).

Entities: carcasses and eggs

An entity is an object with a position that is not an animal (ENV-20). Each has an id, a position and a lifetime in ticks (-1 = none). Its system removes it in the environment phase once it is used up or its lifetime runs out (ENV-22).

Carcasses. When a strike kills, the CarcassSystem leaves a carcass at the killed animal's position. Everything about it comes from the killed species' own Edible:

Edible setting Reference (prey) Meaning
Carcass Portions 2 portions, one each for animals other than the killer (0 = no carcass)
Carcass Energy 30 energy points per portion
Carcass Ticks 100 ticks before it rots away, eaten or not

The killer never eats from its own kill, and each eater takes at most one portion. A species eats carcasses when its diet lists carcass:<species>, and finds them with the "Carcass" sense (NearestEntitySense). Its prompt then gets the carcass system's Prompt Rule: "A kill leaves a carcass that up to {portions} other predators can eat from."

Eggs. Each conception lays one egg per baby in the EggSystem. Eggs have no lifetime: they wait for the hatch phase. With no incubation they hatch in the same tick. In the reference, eggs carry no Edible, so nothing eats them (Reproduction and population).

Random streams

Every draw comes from a named stream (RAND-02). The map uses the world seed, everything that happens during the run uses the run seed:

Stream Seed Used for
world/<cover name>, e.g. world/Thickets world the cover map
world/<layer name>, e.g. world/Grass world the first food items
<layer name>, e.g. Grass run the layer's regrowth, each tick (ENV-03)
<species>/actions run side steps, strike rolls
<species>/founders run where founders and newcomers appear

Turn on the World's Separate World Seed and set its World Seed to keep one map while the run seed varies (RAND-04). Carcasses, eggs and the terrain ground draw no random numbers.

In the editor

  • The run views draw a flat ground with its food and cover cells, and a marker for each entity: a dark red disc per carcass, an ivory ball per egg (Views and real time).
  • The species inspector lists what a species eats and the energy of each target. Window ▸ EvoSim ▸ Food Web shows who eats whom (Editor tooling).
  • Validation reports a fraction outside [0, 1] (V-35), a terrain ground without a terrain (V-20) and diet targets without an Edible (V-24).
