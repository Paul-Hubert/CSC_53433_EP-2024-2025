Remarks

Teaching-path exercise 8. The stamina stat starts full and is capped by the animal's stamina.max trait. In the act phase the locomotion gets Budget(Animal) as the most it may move this tick (through StaminaBudget(Animal)); after the animal acted, the metabolism calls Settle(Animal, float, Energy):

  • moved more than 1 mm: stamina drops by the meters moved;
  • still, and below its stamina.max: stamina rises by the stamina.regen trait (up to the maximum) and the animal pays RegenEnergyCost energy;
  • still and full: nothing.

A busy animal never moves, so it recovers like any still animal. Other modules find stamina with Species.Module<Stamina>(), so a replacement derives from this class.
