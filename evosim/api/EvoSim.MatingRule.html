Remarks

One per species; a species without it never breeds. The breed phase takes, by id, each animal whose current action mates, that IsReady(Animal) and hasn't bred this period, and pairs it with the nearest ready animal of its species within Reach that hasn't bred either (ties by id). When Sexual is false it breeds alone (REPRO-04). The litter, eggs and energy cost follow (Litter).

Kin senses report readiness through the same IsReady(Animal). Overrides of IsAdult(Animal), IsReady(Animal) and Sexual must only read the animal: no change, no random draw.
