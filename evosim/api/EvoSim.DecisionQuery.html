Remarks

The ask-brains phase builds one per deciding animal and passes the ones the memo and the cache could not answer to Ask(IReadOnlyList<DecisionQuery>). A brain reads what it needs: a prompt-based brain calls Prompt(Brain), a numeric one reads Observation and Genes. Tools (prompt preview, integrity gates) build queries too.
