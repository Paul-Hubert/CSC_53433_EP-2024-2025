Examples

T-ACT-06: 10 000 strikes with kill chance 0.5 kill half the time.

Stat.Binomial(kills, n, 0.5, 4, "kills");

Remarks

Random rules are tested by counting over many draws from a fixed seed: the test then passes or fails the same way on every run. k = 4 standard errors (the default) fails a correct rule about once in 16 000 seeds.
