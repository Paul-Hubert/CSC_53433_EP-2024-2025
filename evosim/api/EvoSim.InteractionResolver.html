Remarks

The act phase makes one at initialization, with the reaches of its Inspector, and calls Apply(Animal, in Interaction, Vector3) for every animal that planned an interaction, right after that animal moved: in the act order for the sequential orders, in a fresh random order for the simultaneous one (ACT-30). An animal with an overlay (ACT-07) has two calls in a row, its action's interaction then its overlay's; each is checked on its own. Tests make their own.

Every meal goes through the same steps: the eater's Gain(Animal, float) (after EnergyFrom(Animal, EdibleTarget, float)), one more meal on the animal and its species, and, after a kill or a carcass portion, AfterMeal(Animal, float). A strike's kill chance is drawn from the hunter species' actions stream.
