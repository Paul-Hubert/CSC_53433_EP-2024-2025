The rays of all deciding animals of a sense phase, cast in one batch (SENSE-30) with RaycastCommand, or one at a time with Physics.Raycast (Immediate, for comparison: SENSE-31). Version-specific physics code lives here (ARCH-10).

Remarks

Each BatchedSense adds its rays with Add(Vector3, Vector3, float, int) while CastRays(IReadOnlyList<Animal>) walks the deciding animals; Execute() casts them; Hit(Animal, Sense, int, out RaycastHit) gives the k-th ray of one animal and one sense back. Rays ignore triggers and never hit the views' layer (RAND-11).
