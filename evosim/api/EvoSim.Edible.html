Remarks

Put it on the same GameObject as the Species, the ResourceLayer or the EntityKind it makes edible. The eater's Diet says what it eats; this component says how it is eaten and how much it gives (20 §3.12). A disabled Edible counts as none.

The reference: the Grass layer is grazed for 25; the prey is struck for 60 and leaves a carcass of 2 portions of 30 that rots in 100 ticks; eggs have none.
