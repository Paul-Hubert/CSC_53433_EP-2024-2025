Remarks

A busy animal doesn't decide and doesn't move; it pays the busy cost and recovers stamina, and its busy count drops by one per tick in the act phase (ANIM-30). The interaction resolver calls AfterMeal(Animal, float) after a kill or a portion, and so does Feed(Animal, float, bool) when asked to digest.

Remarks

It sets the busy count to round(digestTicks × gain / referenceGain) ticks, replacing any count the animal had, and the reason to Reason. A count of 0 leaves the animal free.
