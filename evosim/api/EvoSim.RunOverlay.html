Remarks

One of the run views: put it on any GameObject of the scene, outside the World (the "Run views" object of the reference scenes). The run panel sits at the top left with the action colour legend under it (the colours of ActionColors, and white for searching); the followed animal's panel sits at the top right. Text scales with the window's height.

Keys: Tab shows or hides the panels. Clicks on a panel don't reach the camera: the overlay gives Covers(Vector2) to Blocks.
