Energy energy;
public override void Initialize() => energy = Species.Module<Energy>();

float vision = a.Trait(visionTrait);                                                  // a trait, fixed at birth

Remarks

An animal is no GameObject and has no Update: phases loop over the animals of each species (Animals), and a pooled view only shows it. Species modules keep their per-animal values here, as stats and traits they declared (ANIM-10, ANIM-15): read and write a stat with the indexer, a trait with Trait(TraitId) and SetTrait(TraitId, float).

Animals are made by CreateAnimal(string, Vector3, float, Genome, int, int[], Action<Animal>) and join the living with Add(Animal). A dead animal is marked IsGone by RecordDeath(Animal, string); its record stays readable.
