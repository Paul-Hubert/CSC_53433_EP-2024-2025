Remarks

Entry k is the token index the species' k-th sense returned (Index); the species turns it into the situation text with Describe(Observation, TextStyle). Two observations are equal when their tuples are equal: positions, ids and anything else the senses did not report are not part of it.

It is the part of the memo key that changes from decision to decision (DEC-30), so a species with few possible observations gets many memo hits. Read a token by sense label with ObservationView.
