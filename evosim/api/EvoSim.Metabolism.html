Remarks

The act phase calls Settle(Animal, float, float, bool) for each living animal right after it moved and its interaction applied. The energy paid that tick is:

  • moved (more than 1 mm): baseCost + moveCost × meters (reference 0.7 + 0.5 per meter);
  • still and busy at the start of the tick: restCost (0.2);
  • still, with an action that rests (Rests): restCost (0.2);
  • still otherwise: baseCost (0.7);

plus costPerUnit × the cost trait when one is set (09 §5), plus the locomotion's extra cost. Then the species' Stamina pays the meters or recovers (Settle(Animal, float, Energy)); recovering costs RegenEnergyCost more energy per tick (reference 0.3).
