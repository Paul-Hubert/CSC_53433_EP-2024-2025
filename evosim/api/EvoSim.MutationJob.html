One mutation of one gene of one baby (10 §1): its choices are drawn at conception (MUT-30); the result may come later, from the mutator (MUT-32). Succeeded → a new value; failed → the inherited allele stays (MUT-04).

Remarks

StartMutation(Gene, Allele, RandomStream) returns one. An operator that answers at once returns Done(AlleleValue, string, string, long?) or Failed(string). One that works in the background returns Running(Pending) with a Pending the hatch phase can wait for, and later calls Complete(AlleleValue, string, string, long?) or Fail(string); jobs of the MutatorService have no pending of their own: the hatch phase waits on the service.

At hatching a successful job's Result is checked by the gene, then registered as a mutant with Operator, Model and Seed (MUT-03, GENE-12).
