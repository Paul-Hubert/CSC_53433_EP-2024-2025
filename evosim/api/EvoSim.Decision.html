Remarks

The decision phases fill it in turn: the sense phase sets Animal, Observation and Situation; the ask-brains phase the Query, the keys, and the row when the memo or the cache has it, else the Answer to wait for; the choose-actions phase the final Row or the failure. Objects are pooled and reused from tick to tick: don't keep one beyond its tick.
