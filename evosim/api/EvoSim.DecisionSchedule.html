A species' own decision period, and staggering (DEC-04, MAY): an animal decides when (tick + id) % period == 0. Both change behaviour. In the reference scenes every species has one, staggered, with the World's period (owner 2026-10-10); a species without one decides all together, every World period, as the Python prototype does.

Remarks

Place it under a species, at most one: the sense phase asks IsDue(Animal, int, World) for each living, not busy animal. Whatever the schedule says, a busy animal never decides (DEC-02) and an animal without an action decides at once (DEC-01).

Staggering spreads the brain's load: with a period of 4, about a quarter of the animals decide on each tick instead of all of them every fourth tick.

Remarks

Called by the sense phase at the start of every tick. An override must depend only on the tick and the animal's state, and draw no random number, so a seed still repeats a run.
