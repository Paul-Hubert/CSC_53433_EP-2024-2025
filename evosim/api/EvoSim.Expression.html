Remarks

Every gene is expressed in two situations, each with one target only:

  • at birth, by CreateAnimal(string, Vector3, float, Genome, int, int[], Action<Animal>): Animal is the newborn, traits are set, prompt lines are dropped;
  • when the genes block of a decision is written, by BrainGenes(Species, Genome), for the genes the brain reads only: Animal is null, traits are dropped, prompt lines are collected.

So a gene's Express(AlleleValue, Expression) must act only through this object.

Parameters

The value, in the trait's unit, or a factor when multiplyDefault is true.

False: the value replaces the trait. True: the trait becomes its default times the value.
