Remarks

Place it under the species, with the other species modules. When a run starts in Play mode, the World makes a ViewPool from it, with Prewarm views ready. Each instance of the prefab has its colliders disabled and every part moved to the Ignore Raycast layer, so no sense ray ever hits a view. To show more than the reference, put an AnimalView subclass on the prefab's root.
