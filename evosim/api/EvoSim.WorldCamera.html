Class WorldCamera

Namespace

A camera for watching a run: it frames the world at the start; WASD or the arrows pan, the wheel zooms, the right mouse button (or Q and E) orbits, the middle button drags, F frames the world again. A left click on an animal follows it (Esc stops). Picking is done on screen, from the views' positions: views have no colliders (SPACE-12), and the camera only reads the World.

Remarks

Put it on the scene's camera (it needs a Camera on the same GameObject). It orbits a focus point on the ground, at Distance meters, looking down at the Inspector's pitch. Controls, read only while the window has the focus, in real time, so they work while the run is paused or waits:

  • W A S D or the arrow keys: pan, at panSpeed camera distances per second; stops following.
  • Mouse wheel: zoom, from 3 to 2 000 m; not over the overlay's panels.
  • Right mouse button held: orbit (mouse left and right) and tilt (mouse up and down, 10° to 89°).
  • Q and E: orbit left and right, at orbitSpeed degrees per second.
  • Middle mouse button held: drag the ground; stops following.
  • F: frame the whole world again; stops following.
  • Left click (press and release within 5 pixels) on an animal: follow it; Esc: stop following.

The RunOverlay shows the followed animal and keeps clicks on its panels from picking.
