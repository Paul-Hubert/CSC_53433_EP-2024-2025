Remarks

Make one with OfText(string) or OfNumber(float). A sentence has its spaces collapsed at once, so "Eat when hungry. " and "Eat when hungry." are the same value. Two values are equal when they have the same kind and the same Canonical(int) form at 4 decimals; the registry compares numbers at the gene's own precision.
