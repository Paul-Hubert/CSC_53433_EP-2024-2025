Remarks

Place it under the World's Environment object with an Edible (reference: "Grass", grazed, 25 per item); diets and senses name it by its GameObject's name.

Lifecycle: Initialize() lays the grid over the ground; Begin(), once cover exists, picks the cells that may hold food and the first items from the stream "world/<layer name>" (world seed, RAND-04); Regrow(RandomStream) runs once per tick in the environment phase with the layer's own stream (ENV-03).

On a terrain, cells within nearWaterRadius of water regrow nearWaterBonus times faster.
