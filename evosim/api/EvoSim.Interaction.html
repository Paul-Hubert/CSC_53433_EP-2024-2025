The contact an action asks for on arrival (ACT-03, ACT-10): graze an item, strike an animal, eat a carcass portion or an egg, or a custom one. A small value, so planning allocates nothing.

Remarks

Build one with a static method (Graze(ResourceItem), Strike(Animal), Scavenge(Carcass), EatEgg(Egg), Of(IInteraction)) and pass it to OnArrival(Interaction). The InteractionResolver applies it after the animal moved, if the target is within reach, still there, and allowed by the eater's diet (SPEC-13).
