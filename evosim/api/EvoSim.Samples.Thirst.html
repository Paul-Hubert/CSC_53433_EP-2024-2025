Remarks

What it adds. A stat thirst on every animal of the species, from 0 (at birth) to Deadly, and a rule line in the prompt. Thirst itself only declares and describes the stat: it grows, and kills, through DiesOfThirst, and DrinkAction sets it back to 0.

Mechanism. A SpeciesModule that declares a stat in Declare(SpeciesBuilder) and writes a prompt rule in WritePromptRules(PromptWriter). Other modules find it with Species.Module<Thirst>() and read the stat through Value; a LevelSense with stat "thirst" lets the brain sense it, with no code.

In a scene. Add it as a component on a new child GameObject of the species (next to its other stats), with a DiesOfThirst. Checklist of 22 §1: the cause "thirst" appears in death events and in stats.csv (deaths_thirst).

Parameters

b SpeciesBuilder

The species' declarations: call DeclareStat(string, StatStart, float, float, TraitId) and DeclareTrait(string, float, float, float, bool), and keep the ids they return.

Remarks

Called before any module's Initialize: don't look up other modules here. Two modules may declare the same name: they get the same id, and the first declaration counts (V-38 if they differ).

WritePromptRules(PromptWriter)

Adds the rule line "Thirst grows every step; an animal dies of thirst at 100. Drinking resets it.", with the number taken from Deadly (PROMPT-03).

public override void WritePromptRules(PromptWriter w)

Parameters

w PromptWriter

The species' prompt writer: add a line with w.Rule("…").

Remarks

Called while the species' prompt template is built, in module order. The lines go into the prompt's id, its cache keys and the species' results, so keep them stable.
