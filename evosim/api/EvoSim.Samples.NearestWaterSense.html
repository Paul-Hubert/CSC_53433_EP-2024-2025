Remarks

What it adds. A sense labelled "Water" whose tokens are here, one per distance band, and none: "Water: 1-4 meters away." in V1, "I am at the water's edge." when within 0.5 m of the water. Only a ground that knows water answers (NearestWater(Vector3, float): the terrain ground, or a test's pond); on a flat ground it always says none.

Mechanism. A BandedSense: the base class builds the tokens from the bands and writes the texts; the subclass only turns on "here" (HasHere) and maps a distance to a token in Read(Animal, SenseContext).

In a scene. Add it on a new child GameObject among the species' senses: its place among them is its place in the situation text. It multiplies the observation space by its number of tokens (6 with the reference bands).

Remarks

Called by the sense phase, through ReadAll(IReadOnlyList<Animal>, SenseContext, Span<int>), for every animal that decides this tick, and by tools (Observe(Animal, SenseContext)). It must give the same token for the same state, draw no random number, change nothing in the world, and learn about other animals only through s, which leaves out hidden and killed ones (SENSE-03, SENSE-06).
