Distance bands (06 §3, SENSE-20): increasing edges e1 < … < ek and a vision v > ek. A distance falls in the first band with d ≤ ei, in "far" if ek < d ≤ v, and is "none" beyond v. Reference: 1, 4, 10 m and 20 m.

Remarks

A serializable setting of every BandedSense: band 0 is "adjacent" (≤ 1 m), band 1 "close" (1–4 m), band 2 "medium" (4–10 m) and band 3 "far" (10–20 m) in the reference. It also writes distances the way the brain reads them: "within 1 meter", "1-4 meters away", "none within 20 meters" (SENSE-12, SENSE-13). Edges are validated before the run (SENSE-21, V-30).
