What a kill leaves (03 §3): a few portions that other eaters may eat, one portion each, never the killer. It rots away when its lifetime runs out, eaten or not, and goes as soon as its last portion is eaten (ENV-22).

Remarks

Create(Animal, int) makes one when a strike kills, and EatPortion(Carcass, Animal) feeds an eater from it. The system reuses removed carcasses, so don't keep a reference to one after the environment phase removed it.
