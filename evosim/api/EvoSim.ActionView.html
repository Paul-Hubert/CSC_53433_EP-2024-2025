Remarks

It sits on the root of the reference body prefabs (PreyBody, PredatorBody), with its two renderers set. Colours go through a MaterialPropertyBlock, so no material is copied, and a renderer is updated only when its colour changed.

OnShow(Animal)

Colours the marker after the current action (white while searching) and darkens the body with the energy left: full brightness at full energy, hungryBrightness at zero.

public override void OnShow(Animal a)

The animal this view shows.

Remarks

The World calls it from its LateUpdate on the main thread, every frame the view is shown, also while the run is paused or waits for a brain in the Responsive mode. The animal is as the last tick left it. Don't change it, its species or the World, and draw no random number from the simulation's streams.
