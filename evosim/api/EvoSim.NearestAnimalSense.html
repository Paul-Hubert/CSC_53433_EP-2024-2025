The nearest animal of a set (threats, prey, kin, listed species), in bands (06 §4). With readiness on, a kin sense also says whether that animal is ready to mate, when it is within the partner range; beyond it readiness is unknown and not written. Hidden and killed animals are never reported (SENSE-06).

Remarks

The reference of teaching-path exercise 5 (MyNearestAnimalSense, 22 §0). What it does:

  • Tokens (BuildTokens()): for each band in order, "close, ready" and "close, not ready" when readiness is on and part of the band lies within the partner range, then the plain "close" when readiness is off or part of the band lies beyond it; "none" last. The reference threat sense has 5 tokens (4 bands, none), the reference kin sense 9 (4 bands × ready / not ready, none).
  • Read(Animal, SenseContext): the animal's vision trait is the radius; the nearest visible animal of the set (NearestAnimal(Animal, AnimalSet, float, IReadOnlyList<Species>)) gives the band; within the partner range, IsReady(Animal) picks "ready" or "not ready"; nothing found gives "none".
  • Write(int, TextStyle): the banded text ("Animal: 1-4 meters away.") plus ", ready to mate" in V1, or " It is ready to mate." after the sentence in V2.
  • Default label: "Animal" for kin; for threats and prey, the display name of the only species in the set, else "Predator" or "Prey".

The reference prey has two (a threat sense "Predator" and a kin sense "Animal" with readiness); the reference predator has a prey sense "Prey" and a kin sense "Other predator" with readiness.

Remarks

Called by the sense phase, through ReadAll(IReadOnlyList<Animal>, SenseContext, Span<int>), for every animal that decides this tick, and by tools (Observe(Animal, SenseContext)). It must give the same token for the same state, draw no random number, change nothing in the world, and learn about other animals only through s, which leaves out hidden and killed ones (SENSE-03, SENSE-06).

Remarks

The species joins the fragments of its senses with spaces (Describe(Observation, TextStyle)). The text depends on the token only, never on the animal, so the same observation always gives the same text: the answer cache keys on it. Override it for V2 sentences or to state a range in meters (SENSE-12, SENSE-13).
