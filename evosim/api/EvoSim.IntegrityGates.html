The brain and mutator gates of 32 §2 (B-02…B-10), measured as the prototype measured them (e1_sensitivity): genomes from texts (founders, control sentences, the neutral genome, contrast pairs, single edits) × the reference situations (15 §2), through the scene's real prompt and brain. Queries are built from texts only: nothing is registered in the world. Each writes a report (IntegrityReport).

Remarks

Each gate opens the scene additively, makes the named brain the default with no mutation, initialises the world (for its species, prompts and texts), asks the brain directly, and writes its report under Logs/EvoSim/Reports. None is blocking: a failed gate is a finding to track (32 §2). Run them through RunIntegrity() (-checks B-02,B-03…) or RunNamed(string, string, string, bool).

The reference situations come from Data/Observations/prey_observations_v2.jsonl for prey and from ObservationSet.PredatorReference for a species that senses prey.

IntegrityReport

The written report: one row per species and locus with its mean ΔP and sign accuracy.

Remarks

ΔP is P(the gene's action | pro) − P(the same action | anti) in one situation. Passes with a sign accuracy ≥ 0.85 and a mean ΔP ≥ 0.25. A hide gene's situations get cover in sight (see WithCoverInSight).

Locality(string, string)

B-04 G3 locality: 10 parents × 4 single mutations by the scene's mutator; locality ratio ≤ 0.5, Spearman ρ ≥ 0.3.

public static IntegrityReport Locality(string scene, string brainName)

The brain under test: a GameObject name or a brain id.

Returns

IntegrityReport

The written report, with the ratio, ρ and the number of edits in its facts.

Remarks

On the first species only. The locality ratio is the median behaviour distance parent → child over the median distance between unrelated parents: a small edit should change behaviour less than a new genome. ρ correlates text distance with behaviour distance. Needs the mutator server.
