Class PondGround

Namespace
EvoSim.Testing
Assembly
EvoSim.Testing.dll

A test ground: flat, with a round pond (not walkable) whose shore animals can reach (samples 22 §2–§3). It answers the water queries that the water sense and the drink action ask.

NearestWater(Vector3, float)

The pond seen from p: its water just inside the edge, its shore just outside (1 cm each way), and the distance to the edge (0 in the pond); null when the edge is farther than radius.

public override WaterHit? NearestWater(Vector3 p, float radius)

Remarks

The drink action walks to the shore point; the water sense reports the distance.
