Remarks

The map is built once, in Initialize(), after the ground: the noise comes from the stream "world/<GameObject name>", which uses the world seed, so runs that share a world seed share their thickets (RAND-04). Cover never changes during a run, except by SetCover(Vector3, bool) and Clear().

The food layer reads it in its Begin() to keep cover cells free of food ("hungry cover", ENV-04).
