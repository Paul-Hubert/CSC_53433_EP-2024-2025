A conceived baby waiting to hatch (REPRO-20): its parents, generation, genome after crossover, the mutations still running, and when it hatches. Not an animal: it doesn't sense, decide, move or count toward caps (REPRO-22).

Remarks

The breed phase fills one per baby and lays it with Lay(Egg); the hatch phase turns it into an animal at HatchTick, once IsComplete (MUT-32).
