A Unity Terrain as ground (02 §1): heights from the heightmap; walkable above the water level, below a maximum steepness, outside tree footprints; only the largest connected walkable region is kept (SPACE-05). Walkability is computed once, on a grid of cells.

Remarks

Put it on the World's "Ground" GameObject with a Terrain on it or below it. Each cell is judged at its centre: at or below the water level it is water; steeper than the maximum it is blocked; otherwise walkable. Then tree footprints are blocked, and pockets cut off from the largest region are dropped. Water and steep cells are never walkable.

The terrain must not change during a run: the grid is built once, in Initialize().
