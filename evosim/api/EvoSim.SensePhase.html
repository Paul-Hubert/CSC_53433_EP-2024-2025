Remarks

First phase of the reference tick. A species' DecisionSchedule says which ticks are its decision ticks; without one, the World's decision period does (every DecisionPeriod ticks). An animal with no action yet (a newborn, an animal whose busy time just ended) decides at once (REPRO-13).

For each deciding animal it fills a Decision in Decisions: the sense tokens (its observation) and their text (its situation). Senses are read species by species, one sense at a time over the whole group (ReadAll(IReadOnlyList<Animal>, SenseContext, Span<int>), ARCH-12). It only reads the world and draws no random number.
