What an action wants this tick (ACT-03): a direction (or none: stay), walk or run, and the farthest it should go (to stop at a target). The locomotion decides what actually happens (MOVE-01).

Remarks

Actions don't build intents themselves: they call the ActContext intent methods (WalkTo, RunTo, RunAwayFrom, Stay, Wander, Search), which use the static methods below. A Locomotion reads the intent in Move(Animal, Intent, float, MoveContext) and may also change it, for example with Stay() when the ground ahead is too steep (22 §14).
