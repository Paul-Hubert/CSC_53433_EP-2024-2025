Remarks

The reference hunt action of the predator, and teaching-path exercise 12. Each tick it:

  • finds the nearest visible prey (NearestAnimal(Animal, AnimalSet, IReadOnlyList<Species>) with Prey: not killed, not hidden in cover) and the nearest carcass it may eat (NearestCarcass(Animal));
  • if there is a carcass at least as near as the prey (or no prey), runs to it, stopping stopAt meters away, and scavenges it on arrival (Scavenge(Carcass));
  • else, if there is prey, runs to it and strikes it on arrival (Strike(Animal));
  • with neither in sight, searches (ACT-04).

The strike kills with the hunter's kill chance (KillChanceAgainst(Animal, Animal)), at most once per tick (ACT-12); a kill or a portion makes the hunter busy digesting (Digestion).

Act(Animal, ActContext)

Runs to the nearer of the nearest prey and the nearest edible carcass (the carcass on a tie) and strikes or scavenges on arrival; searches when neither is in sight (ACT-04).

public override void Act(Animal a, ActContext c)

Parameters

a Animal

The animal that acts: alive and not busy.

c ActContext

The context to query and plan through; Begin(Animal) was already called for a.

Remarks

Called by the act phase, through ActAll(IReadOnlyList<Animal>, ActContext, Span<ActPlan>), once per tick for each animal that chose this action. Look the target up again each tick, so that a moving target is followed (ACT-02). The plan is the last intent and the last interaction the method set; doing nothing means stay.
